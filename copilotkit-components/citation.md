# Citation

**Official documentation:** https://docs.copilotkit.ai/generative-ui/components/standard-catalog/citation
**Catalog:** https://docs.copilotkit.ai/generative-ui/components/standard-catalog
**Group:** Composites
**Source page:** https://docs.copilotkit.ai/generative-ui/components/standard-catalog/citation

## Purpose

An inline source marker (\[1\]) showing title, snippet and link on hover or click. Put it right after the sentence it supports, as a child of a Text or in a Row.
